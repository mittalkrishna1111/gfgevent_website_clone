How I Made My Marvel Website Using Vibecoding

A simple, plain-English guide to explain your project with 100% confidence in front of club seniors and interviewers.

1. What is "Vibecoding"? (In Everyday English)
💡 The Movie Director Analogy

Imagine you are a movie director. You do not carry the heavy cameras or sew the superhero costumes yourself. Instead, you have the grand story in your head. You tell your talented team exactly what superhero scene to create, how the colors should look, and what feelings you want. When they show you the scene, you review it, test it, and tell them how to make it even cooler.

That is Vibecoding: You are the master director. The AI computer is your super-fast assistant who types the code. You give clear instructions, review every button and picture, and steer the project until it is perfect.

The 3 Golden Rules of Vibecoding:
1. Dream it first: You decide what the website is for (a Marvel hackathon for our university).
2. Describe it clearly: You explain in simple steps what every section and button should do.
3. Test and improve: You click every feature in your browser, find things to polish, and guide the AI to fix them.

2. The Master Prompt (The Exact Words I Gave to the Computer)
COPY & READ THIS IN THE INTERVIEW

"Build a superhero hackathon website for Bennett University students inspired by Marvel's Avengers.

  • Look & Colors: Use a dark space background with Iron Man red, bright gold, cosmic blue, and emerald green. Make it feel like an Iron Man computer screen.
  • Hero Welcome: Show a giant title 'Multiverse of Code 2026' with a live ticking clock counting down to the hackathon day.
  • 5 Superhero Teams: Let students pick a team (Iron Man, Captain America, Doctor Strange, Black Panther, or Thor). Clicking a team changes the website colors.
  • 6 Infinity Stone Topics: Create 6 cards for different competition topics with problem challenges.
  • Live ID Pass Maker: Create a fun form where students type their name and pick a superhero sticker, and their official ID badge updates on screen instantly as they type.
  • Cool Superhero Tricks: Add an Iron Man targeting scanner that follows the mouse, fun button sound effects made right in the browser without downloading any music files, and a golden Thanos glove that makes cards turn into dust when clicked."

Why this prompt is so powerful: It does not just say "make a website." It tells the computer the exact university, the theme, the colors, what every single button does, and all the fun surprises to include!

3. The 3 Easy Steps of Making It (My Process)
1
Step 1: Setting Up the Big Picture & The Information Notebook

I started by telling the AI to build the main skeleton: the top title, the hackathon rules, the prize amounts, and the schedule. I made sure all the event details were stored neatly in one separate "information notebook" file so anyone can change a prize or date in 5 seconds without messing up the design.

2
Step 2: Making It Fun & Interactive (Buttons That Work)

A website shouldn't just be a boring poster. I instructed the computer to connect all the buttons: clicking a superhero team changes the theme color, clicking a topic card pops up a mission card, and typing in the registration form instantly draws a personalized superhero ID card on the screen.

3
Step 3: Adding the "Wow" Superhero Vibes & Testing

In the final step, I added elements that make people smile: Iron Man beep and blast sounds, an animated mouse pointer that looks like a high-tech scanner, a party-popper celebration when you get your pass, and a secret Thanos glove that turns half the screen into dust!

4. Five Cool Things to Brag About in Your Interview

🔊 1. Magic Sound Effects

There are no downloaded song files or audio clips that could break or load slowly. The browser creates the beeps, clicks, and laser sounds live, just like an electronic piano inside your computer!

🪪 2. Live Superhero ID Badge

When you type your name, pick your college, and choose a hero seal, the ID card on the right updates instantly like a mirror. When you click 'Claim Pass', celebratory confetti blasts across the screen!

🤌 3. The Thanos Snap Secret

In the bottom-right corner sits a golden Infinity Gauntlet. Click it, and half the cards on your screen dissolve into cosmic dust! Click the green Time Stone button to bring everything back safely.

🎯 4. Iron Man Targeting Mouse

The mouse pointer isn't an ordinary arrow. It turns into a high-tech glowing scanner circle. Whenever you hover over an item, it locks on and shows a floating tag telling you what it is.

⚡ 5. Runs Anywhere with Just One Double-Click

You don't need to install any heavy software, node modules, or special developer tools. The entire project is split into 5 neat, clean files that open instantly in any web browser like Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

Key Interview Tip: Interviewers love students who think about the user experience. Tell them: "I wanted the website to feel like stepping into Tony Stark's lab rather than reading a boring brochure."

5. Word-For-Word Interview Cheat Sheet
Q1: "Tell us about this website in 30 seconds."
"I designed a superhero hackathon website for our college called Multiverse of Code 2026. It has 5 superhero teams, 6 competition topics, a live countdown clock, a real-time superhero ID card maker, custom button sound effects, and a secret Thanos snap trick. I created it using vibecoding by acting as the creative director and guiding an AI assistant to build it."
Q2: "Did the computer just do everything, or what was your role?"
"The computer typed the lines of code, but I drove the whole project. An AI doesn't know what makes a college hackathon exciting. I chose the Bennett University theme, planned the 5 superhero factions, decided how the live ID pass should work, tested every interaction in the browser, and instructed the AI to add sound effects and the Thanos easter egg."
Q3: "How does the live ID card generator work?"
"It acts like a real-time mirror. Whenever you type a letter in the name box or pick a hero sticker like Spider-Man or Iron Man, the browser catches that change instantly and writes it on the digital ID card. When you click Submit, it plays a laser sound and fires colorful confetti."
Q4: "What is your biggest takeaway from this activity?"
"Vibecoding taught me that the biggest superpower in tech today is clear thinking and communication. If you know what you want to build and can describe it clearly with patience, you can bring any creative idea to life in hours instead of weeks."
6. Your Live Demo Checklist (Show This on Your Laptop)
Step 1: The Teams Click on Thor or Doctor Strange to show how the website colors change.
Step 2: The ID Pass Type the interviewer's name in the box and show the live card update + confetti!
Step 3: The Snap Click the golden glove at the bottom to turn half the cards into dust!

🌟 Final Encouragement: Speak clearly, keep a friendly smile, and enjoy showing off your work. You built an amazing, playful project that will stand out from everyone else!
